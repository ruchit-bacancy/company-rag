# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave

Every full-time employee receives 18 paid leaves per calendar year.

Unused annual leaves can be carried forward to the following year, up to a maximum of 10 days.

## Casual Leave

Employees can use casual leave for personal requirements or short-term situations.

Casual leave should normally be requested at least one working day in advance.

## Sick Leave

Employees who are unable to work due to illness can request sick leave.

For sick leave of more than two consecutive working days, employees may be asked to provide supporting documentation.

## Emergency Leave

In an emergency situation, employees should inform their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

Emergency leave requests can be submitted after the employee returns to work.

## Leave Approval

Leave requests should be submitted through the company's employee portal.

The employee's manager is responsible for approving or rejecting leave requests.
