# Employee Reimbursement Policy

## General Policy

Acme Technologies reimburses employees for reasonable business expenses incurred while performing their job responsibilities.

All reimbursement requests must be submitted through the employee portal.

## Travel Expenses

Employees can claim reimbursement for business-related travel expenses.

Eligible travel expenses include:

- Air or train tickets
- Local transportation
- Hotel accommodation
- Meals during approved business travel

Employees should keep receipts for all eligible expenses.

## Meal Expenses

Employees traveling for business can claim up to ₹800 per day for meals.

Meal expenses above this limit require additional approval from the department manager.

## Internet Reimbursement

Employees working remotely can claim up to ₹1,000 per month for internet expenses.

The employee must provide a valid internet bill with the reimbursement request.

## Submission Deadline

Reimbursement requests should be submitted within 30 days of the expense.

Requests submitted after 30 days may require additional approval from the Finance team.

## Processing Time

Approved reimbursement requests are normally processed within 7 working days.
