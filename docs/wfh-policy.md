# Work From Home Policy

## Eligibility

Full-time employees who have completed their probation period can request work from home.

Employees who are still in their probation period require manager approval for each work-from-home request.

## Regular Work From Home

Employees can work from home up to two days per week.

Work-from-home days should be discussed with the employee's manager in advance.

## Friday Work From Home

Friday is the preferred work-from-home day for most teams.

Employees can work from home on Friday without using their annual leave, provided their manager has approved the arrangement.

## Temporary Work From Home

Employees can request temporary work from home due to personal circumstances, travel, or other reasonable requirements.

The manager may approve temporary work-from-home arrangements depending on project requirements.

## Availability

Employees working from home must remain available during core working hours from 10:30 AM to 5:30 PM.

Employees are expected to attend scheduled meetings and respond to work-related communication during working hours.
