# Company Overview

## About Acme Technologies

Acme Technologies is a software company used for this demonstration project.
The company builds web and mobile applications for small and medium-sized
businesses.

## Working Hours

The standard working day is from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday.

Core working hours, during which all employees are expected to be available,
are from 10:30 AM to 5:30 PM.

## Offices

Acme Technologies has offices in Ahmedabad and Pune. Each employee is assigned
to a home office but may work from home in line with the work from home policy.

## Employee Portal

Most day-to-day requests — including leave requests, reimbursement requests, and
work-from-home requests — are submitted through the company's employee portal.

## Getting Help

For questions that are not covered by a specific policy, employees can contact
the People Operations team through the employee portal.
